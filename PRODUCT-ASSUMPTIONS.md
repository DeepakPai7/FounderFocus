1)What product are you planning to build?
Our plan is to develop FounderFocus - A Smart Startup and Productivity Management Platform.
FounderFocus is an internet-based application that can be used by  budding entrepreneurs, and founders of startups for managing their tasks, prioritizing their work, meeting deadlines, and checking productivity from one single platform.
2)What problem does it solve?
FounderFocus solves the problem of poor task management and lack of focus among students, aspiring entrepreneurs, and startup founders.
People often have multiple tasks, deadlines, and responsibilities but struggle to decide what to work on first. They may forget deadlines, lose track of pending work, or spend time on less important tasks while urgent tasks remain unfinished.
3)Who are the target users?
Primary target audience: Students, aspiring entrepreneurs, and early-stage startup founders
4)What is the main use case?
The main use case of FounderFocus is helping students and aspiring startup founders decide what tasks they should focus on, manage their deadlines, and track their daily progress.
Example scenario
Imagine a entrepreneur managing a startup project. They have several tasks:
- Complete project documentation.
- Prepare a presentation.
- Fix website bugs.
- Submit an assignment.
5)What assumptions are you making about the users?
For our project, the assumptions are:
1. Users are mainly aspiring entrepreneurs and early-stage startup founders.
2. Users have multiple tasks and responsibilities to manage.
3. Users sometimes struggle to decide which task is most important.
4. Users need reminders of deadlines and help managing their time.
5. Users prefer a simple and easy-to-use interface.
6. Users want to monitor their productivity and completed work.
7. Users are comfortable using a web application to manage their tasks.
8. Users are willing to enter their tasks, deadlines and priority levels manually.
6)What assumptions are you making about the users?
User assumptions
1. Start-up founders: Users will be individuals that initiate/start or manage a start-up company, particularly new start-up founders.

2. Limited time and money: Users might possess a limited amount of time, money, and members of the team to manage their start-up activities.

3. Managing tasks is difficult: Users will face problems in organizing their day-to-day tasks and managing them.

4. Prioritizing tasks: They do not know which task to complete first since they might be unaware of which task is more important.

5. Goal driven: Users are goal-oriented and want to accomplish certain business objectives like creating an MVP, customer acquisition, generating revenue, etc.

6. Procrastinators: Users will delay their task due to workload, confusion, or lack of motivation.

7. Limited technical knowledge: Users may not possess much advanced technical/project management skills.

8. Progress tracking: Users want to know about the progress they made and how much work is left pending.
7)What will be included in your MVP?
1. Dashboard
- Overall view of total, done, pending, and overdue tasks.
- Daily Top 3 Priorities.
- Task creation at once.
- Coming tasks overview.
2. Task Management
- Create, update, and remove tasks.
- Set deadlines and priority level (high, medium, low).
- Set business impact and efforts.
- Mark tasks as done.
- Postpone tasks and monitor postponement history.
- Search for and filter tasks.
3. Smart Task Prioritization
- Prioritize tasks automatically depending on priority, business impact, deadlines, and efforts.
- Shows top 3 tasks that founders need to concentrate on.
- Specifies reasons for each task being chosen.
4. Business Goals Management
- Create business goals.
- Give description and set dates for achieving them.
- Associate tasks with business goals.
- Monitor goal achievement using progress bars.
5. Progress Monitoring
- Weekly statistics about task completion.
- Number of completed and pending tasks.
- Task completion rate.
- Overview of coming 7 days' tasks.
6. Data Persistence
- Store tasks and business goals in browser localStorage.
- Keep data despite refreshing page.
8)What will NOT be included in the MVP?
Feature	Why it is excluded
AI-powered task recommendations	The initial version uses rule-based prioritization.
Team collaboration	MVP focuses on individual startup founders.
Multiple user accounts	Authentication and account management are outside the initial scope.
Real-time notifications	Not essential to test the core product idea.
Calendar integration	Founders can manually enter task deadlines.
Email and SMS reminders	Can be introduced in a later version.
Financial management	Budgeting and expense tracking are not the core focus.
Advanced analytics	Basic progress statistics are sufficient initially.
Cloud synchronization	Initial data storage uses browser localStorage.
Mobile application	The MVP will be a responsive web application.
Third-party integrations	External tools such as Slack, Trello, and Google Calendar are excluded.
Subscription and payment system	The MVP will not include monetization features.
